Hello!

Maps, data, and the people they serve

I'm a Geospatial Data Scientist who turns satellite imagery and spatial data into tools, maps and decisions. At the United Nations (ITU/Giga) I build data pipelines, run infrastructure analysis across countries, and trained generative AI models on satellite imagery for my master's thesis.

I also love the human side of the work: leading the development of a global knowledge platform, engaging ministries and regulators across 14 countries, and speaking at international forums such as the AI for Good Global Summit. I work in English, Spanish and Polish, and I care about technology that actually reaches people.
